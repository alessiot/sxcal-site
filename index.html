Your data stays yours

SX/Cal uses your own OpenAI API key — your requests go straight from your device to OpenAI, nothing in between.

No server

Your calendar, notes, and reminders are stored on your device. There isn't a SX/Cal server for them to live on.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking of any kind — not a reduced version, not present at all.

Nothing sold

Your data is never sold or shared beyond what you send OpenAI to understand your own requests.
